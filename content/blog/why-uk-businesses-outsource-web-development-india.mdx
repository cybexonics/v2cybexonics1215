## What UK Businesses Get Right by Outsourcing

### Access to Full-Stack Teams
When you hire a UK freelancer at £400–£600/day you get one person. When you partner with an Indian IT company you get a full team — developer, designer, QA, and project manager — for the same budget.

### Modern Technology Stack
Top Indian IT companies like Cybexonics work with the same stack as leading UK agencies — Next.js, React, Node.js, AWS, TypeScript. There is no technology gap in 2025.

### IST and GMT Overlap
IST overlaps with UK working hours in the afternoon. A message sent at 9am UK time arrives in India at 2:30pm IST — same day response every day.

## What to Watch Out For

**Communication** — Do they respond quickly and speak clear English?

**Portfolio** — Have they built projects similar to yours?

**Process** — Do they use project management tools and provide clear timelines?

**Post-launch support** — Will they be available after your site goes live?
