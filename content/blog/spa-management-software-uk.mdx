## How it works: step by step

Here is the exact journey from the moment you open the software to completing a client treatment and recording payment.

### Step 1: Sign-in and spa name setup

When you first launch Cybexonics Spa OS, you are greeted with a simple onboarding prompt. You enter your name and the trading name of your spa.

As soon as you submit this, the system customises the workspace. Your spa name appears directly in the persistent header bar across every screen. Because it is white-label software, your therapists and visiting clients see your business identity rather than third-party vendor logos.

### Step 2: Adding a customer record

Client care starts with reliable records. When a client rings to book or walks through your door, click the **Add Customer** button.

A focused form lets you input the client's essential contact details and create their profile. In our free live demo, you can add up to 5 customer records to see how the system handles real client profiles without entering payment details.

### Step 3: Booking a treatment (60/90 minutes)

Treatment timings in wellness and day spas centre on structured intervals. Rather than forcing you to configure intricate schedules, Cybexonics Spa OS provides dedicated treatment duration options:

1. **60-minute treatments** (ideal for standard massages, bespoke facials, or express wellness rituals).
2. **90-minute treatments** (designed for extended body therapies, holistic rituals, and combination packages).

You select the client from your customer list, pick the appointment slot, choose either the 60-minute or 90-minute treatment duration, and confirm the booking. The diary updates clearly with the chosen appointment duration.

[SCREENSHOT: Treatment appointment booking dialog highlighting 60-minute and 90-minute duration selector]
*Image alt text: Treatment booking window in Cybexonics Spa OS showing clear toggle buttons for 60-minute and 90-minute appointment durations.*

### Step 4: Taking payment (cash or online) and applying discounts

When the client finishes their treatment and comes to your reception counter, checking them out is direct and transparent.

Open the client's booking and choose their payment mode:

* **Cash:** For clients paying in notes or coins at your counter.
* **Online:** For transactions processed through card terminals or online payment links.

If you are running a seasonal promotion, honouring a loyalty voucher, or offering a package discount, you can apply the discount directly on this screen before completing the transaction. The final balance recalculates automatically, keeping your end-of-day tally accurate.

[SCREENSHOT: Payment checkout modal with cash versus online payment selector and discount input field]
*Image alt text: Payment checkout dialog in Cybexonics Spa OS displaying cash and online payment options alongside a discount entry box.*

### Step 5: Managing secure staff access

In a busy spa, therapists and front-desk coordinators need access to the booking calendar, but you still need full administrative control over who signs in.

Cybexonics Spa OS uses **Google Sign-In** paired with a **staff whitelist**. Only email addresses specifically approved by the spa owner can log into the workspace. Staff members whose Google email is on the approved list can sign in, ensuring unapproved accounts cannot access the system.

[SCREENSHOT: Staff access settings screen showing Google sign-in status and approved email whitelist list]
*Image alt text: Staff access panel in Cybexonics Spa OS displaying the Google sign-in configuration and staff email whitelist.*

### Step 6: Viewing customer records

Your client details remain stored in your customer records view. You can review customer details, verify payment modes, and keep track of your client list without relying on paper notebooks.

The software runs in any modern web browser on your reception desktop or tablet. In the full production version, an Android mobile application is also provided so managers can check records on mobile devices.
